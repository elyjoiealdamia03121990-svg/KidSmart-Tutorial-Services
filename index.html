Questions

Is the AI safe for my child?

The tutor only talks about the lesson. There is no open chat, it never asks for personal information, and your child's name stays on your device. We still recommend a parent nearby, especially for the little ones.

What device do we need?

Any phone, tablet or computer with Chrome, Safari or Edge, and the sound turned on. Tablets are best for small hands.

How long should my child use it?

About 15 to 30 minutes a day is plenty. The app stops for the day once the daily limit is reached.

Does it replace school?

No. It is practice and support at home - like a patient tutor who helps your child keep up and build confidence.
